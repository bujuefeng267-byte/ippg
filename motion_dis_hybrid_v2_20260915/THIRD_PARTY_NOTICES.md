# 第三方来源

DIS/PBV/POS 的投影公式与预处理参考 Xiao 等人的官方 `get_rppg.m`，Python 适配明确记录在 `dis_core.py`。

- 论文：Xiao, Chang; Tan, Chengyifeng; Song, Lixia; Lu, Hongzhou; Wang, Wenjin. *Camera-based cardio-respiratory monitoring across the full fitness cycle*. Physiological Measurement, 2025. https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6579/adc364
