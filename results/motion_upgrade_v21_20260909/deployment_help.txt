usage: analyze_motion_v2.py [-h] --output OUTPUT [--cache CACHE]
                            [--max-seconds MAX_SECONDS] [--max-gap MAX_GAP]
                            [--window WINDOW] [--step STEP]
                            [--min-bpm MIN_BPM] [--max-bpm MAX_BPM]
                            [--reference-ubfc REFERENCE_UBFC]
                            video

V2.1 offline multi-ROI rPPG with HR derived from the saved fused waveform. No
reference HR is supplied to extraction, waveform fusion or candidate
selection. The optional reference is applied only AFTER all predictions have
been produced. Both filtering/overlap fusion and the final DP tracker are
offline.

positional arguments:
  video

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --output OUTPUT
  --cache CACHE         Reuse an exact V2 frontend trace with matching
                        identity
  --max-seconds MAX_SECONDS
  --max-gap MAX_GAP
  --window WINDOW
  --step STEP
  --min-bpm MIN_BPM
  --max-bpm MAX_BPM
  --reference-ubfc REFERENCE_UBFC
